# Other public projects by the same author

What an agent (a coding agent working on this repository, or the companion herself) may say about the other public repositories of the owner (`github.com/rhedak`). Say only what is here, link to it, and say when something is unofficial or AI-made. Check `gh repo list rhedak --visibility public` before you add a line: a repository that is private or archived is not listed.

## Draconia Malfoid

An unofficial, non-commercial fan character (a 21-year-old adult woman, loosely inspired by Draco Malfoy; not affiliated with Warner Bros. or Wizarding World). Her picture packs are in [anime_ai_companion-packs](https://github.com/rhedak/anime_ai_companion-packs) (not MIT, see each pack's `NOTICE.md`). Two repositories show more of her, and the companion points to them while Draconia's pack is on:

| Repository | What it is | Where to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [malfoid-gallery](https://github.com/rhedak/malfoid-gallery) | Seasonal and social-media images of her, made in Grok Imagine, each traceable to its prompt and base picture. Its README links the demo with her pack on: `…/anime_ai_companion/demo/?pack=draconia-uniform`. | <https://rhedak.github.io/malfoid-gallery/> |
| [malfoid-the-girl-who-yearned](https://github.com/rhedak/malfoid-the-girl-who-yearned) | *Malfoid: The Girl who Yearned*, a free fan webcomic that retells *Philosopher's Stone* from Harry's and Draconia's points of view. Made with AI image tools, lettered by hand. Only the reader and the published panels are public. | <https://rhedak.github.io/malfoid-the-girl-who-yearned/> |

Where this is used: `VOICES.draconia` in `public/script.js` (her `elsewhere` answer, her intro and help in the demo and in basic mode), the `packs/characters/draconia.txt` description (what the real app tells a model), and `test/browser-demo.test.ts`. A new link goes into all three.
