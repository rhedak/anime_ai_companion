# Franzi (casual) picture pack

An original character: **Franzi**, in a casual outfit: a pastel pink cardigan over a white T-shirt and a cream pleated skirt, with a red bow clip at her ponytail. No real person is shown.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI.** The neutral base picture was made with an AI image tool (xAI's Grok Imagine) as a casual-outfit version of Franzi. The six emotions (`happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`,
`shy`, `thinking`) and the four poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) were made by editing that neutral base picture in the same tool and are used as they came, scaled to 940 × 1070. The blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three
mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of the seven emotions were **not** made by an image tool: a script drew
closed eyes and an open mouth over the face of each emotion picture, and left every other pixel identical. They
are simpler than the hand-finished pictures of the other packs.

The background of every picture is transparent. It was removed with a script from the plain black background the pictures were made on: black pixels connected to the picture's edge, and the black gaps between her arms and body, were made transparent.
