## What it is

Fan-made, **unofficial** pictures of Hatsune Miku (初音ミク), a character of **Crypton Future Media, INC.**
(original design by KEI). They are not made, endorsed or approved by Crypton, and they are not an official
product. The pack is a derivative drawing in a chibi style, not a copy of any official illustration.

## What you have to follow

This project **cannot give you the right to use the character**: the licence forbids sublicensing (Article
3(4)). When you use this pack you are using the character under Crypton's licence yourself, so you are the one
who has to follow it. The main points, as I read the translated text (this is not legal advice, and the Japanese
text prevails):

- **Non-commercial only.** No commercial use, and no non-commercial use for a fee or reward. Earning money
  from fan works needs Crypton's "Piapro Link"; anything commercial needs Crypton's permission.
- **Do not harm the character or its creators.** Nothing that damages the reputation of the character or of
  the original creators, nothing defamatory, insulting, obscene, or against public order and morals, and
  nothing that infringes someone else's rights.
- **No advertising or promotion**, and do not pass the work off as your own or as an official Crypton product.
- **Give the credit** above, in a place people can easily see it.
- The licence ends **automatically if you break it** (Article 7), and Crypton can change it (Article 6): use
  the current version, not the one summarised here.

If your project is commercial, or you are unsure, **remove this folder** (`packs/miku`) from your copy. The
rest of the repository is MIT-licensed and works without it.

## How it was made, and a caveat about AI

A base picture of the character was drawn by the repository's owner; the expression, blink and mouth pictures
were then edited from it with an AI image model (xAI's Grok Imagine), and put together by a script that keeps
everything but the face identical between pictures. The licence and Crypton's guidelines say nothing about
AI-generated fan works. **The repository's owner has asked Crypton about this pack** (a message through Crypton's
contact form, in October 2026) **and had no answer when this was written.** If you want certainty for your own use,
ask Crypton yourself through their contact form. If Crypton or a rights holder asks for the pack to be removed, it will be.

**The wave and cheer pictures are temporary stand-ins.** They were made separately, in a different drawing of her than the rest of this pack, and cropped to fit, so they do not match the other pictures yet. They are to be redone from this pack's own pictures.
