# Franzi (pajamas) picture pack

An original character: **Franzi**, in pajamas: a lavender short-sleeved button-up top with a small lapel collar, lavender frilled shorts and white slippers, with her black hair in a high ponytail (no bow). No real person is shown.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI.** The neutral base picture was made with an AI image tool (xAI's Grok Imagine) by editing the casual-outfit neutral base picture of Franzi into the pajamas, and was then cropped and scaled by script to the same framing as the casual base. The six emotions (`happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`,
`shy`, `thinking`) and the four poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) were made by editing that neutral base picture in the same tool and are used as they came, scaled to 940 × 1070 (the `cheer-1` picture came out smaller and was enlarged by script first, so her figure is smaller and slightly softer there). The blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three
mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of the seven emotions were **not** made by an image tool: a script drew
closed eyes and an open mouth over the face of each emotion picture, and left every other pixel identical. They
are simpler than the hand-finished pictures of the other packs.

The background of every picture is transparent. It was removed with a script from the plain black background the pictures were made on: black pixels connected to the picture's edge, and the black gaps between her arms and body, were made transparent.
