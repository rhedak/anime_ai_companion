# Franzi (dress) picture pack

An original character: **Franzi**, in a green dirndl with a red apron. No real person is shown.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI.** The base picture and the six emotions (`happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`,
`shy`, `thinking`) and the four poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) were made with an AI image tool
(xAI's Grok Imagine) and are used as they came, scaled to 940 × 1070. The blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three
mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of the seven emotions were **not** made by an image tool: a script drew
closed eyes and an open mouth over the face of each emotion picture, and left every other pixel identical. They
are simpler than the hand-finished pictures of the other packs.

The background of every picture is plain black, not transparent. It was not removed.

## A caveat about AI-made pictures

Whether pictures made by an image model can be copyrighted, and by whom, is not settled everywhere, and the
