# Risa (dress) picture pack

An original character: **Risa**, the same character as the drawn Risa preset of the
[character creator](https://github.com/rhedak/anime-character-creator), in a green dress. She does not belong to any
company or franchise, and no real person is shown. These pictures are under the **MIT licence** of the rest of the
repository, so you may use, change and share them, commercially too.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI.** The neutral base picture was made with xAI's Grok Imagine by editing Risa's adult
reference picture (`ref/risa-adult` in the `anime-character-creator` repository, the same base as the `risa-bikini` pack),
changing only her outfit to a green sleeveless dress. The six other emotions (`happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`, `shy`,
`thinking`) and the four poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) were made by editing that dress base in the same
tool, one change at a time, and are used as they came, scaled to 940 × 1070 (`cheer-2` came out smaller and was enlarged
first, so it is a little softer, and the tool zoomed out on it, so her figure is smaller there). None of the pictures was
retouched by hand. The blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of the seven
emotions were made by a script over the face of each emotion picture, which left every other pixel identical. It drew the
closed eyes and, for six emotions, the open mouths. The `happy` mouths are the mouth of one more edit of the `happy` picture
with her mouth open, pasted in and, for the two smaller openings, squashed. 39 pictures in all. `PROCESS.md` has the details. The character's design
itself began from AI-generated reference images, as the About page of the documentation says.

The background of every picture is transparent. It was removed with a script from the plain black background the pictures
were made on.

## A caveat about AI-made pictures

Whether pictures made by an image model can be copyrighted, and by whom, is not settled everywhere, and the
terms of the service that made them (xAI's, for Grok Imagine) apply to the output as well. The MIT licence grants
whatever rights the repository's owner has in these pictures; it cannot grant more than exists. This is not legal
advice. If you build something commercial from them and the answer matters to you, check the terms of the tool
yourself, or make your own pack: `docs/frame-packs.md` says how.
